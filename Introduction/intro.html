Around the World of Music

Store Information

Gina D'Angelo, owner of Around the World Music, is an avid music fan. She loves music from many different cultures and locations around the world. Around the World Music offfers a collection of new and used CDs, DVDs, and vinyl records.

Around the World music has a listening station so you can listen to the CDs before you make your purchasing decision. If we don't have what you're looking for, we will order it! Special orders require prepayment.

Popular Annual Music Festival

  1. New Orleans Jazz and Heritage Festival (New Orleans, LA) - last week in April
  2. Chichago Blues Festival (Chicago, IL) - first week of May
  3. Summerfest (Milwaukee, WI) - begins last Thursday in June
  4. Ittawa BlueFest (Ottawa, Canada) - first week in July
  5. Newport Music Festival (Newport, RI) - mid-July
