Phainon Khaslana

Timeline of his Flamechase Journey

Cycle 0

  • An innocent child from Aedes Elysiae, who sought to become the hero of his village, who's only wish to fulfill everyone's wishes.
  • Eventually, his village will be subjected to an attack from the black tide, an unknown entity from beyond his world.
  • He defended his village from monsters from the black tide only using an ordinary rake. But everything was in vain when everyone he fought for died in the attack.
  • As a Chrysos Heir, he was pushed to become the Deliverer of Amphoreus. Together with his friend, Cyrene, they journeyed to Ohkema to train.
  • Together with the rest of the Chrysos Heirs, they seized the Coreflames of the Titans.
  • Darkness still consumed Amphoreus, leaving Khaslana and Cyrene to lose hope in the so called "dawn" of Amphoreus.
  • Eventually, they learned from the Theoros, Lycurgus, that their world is nothing more than just a simulation, a testing ground for killing the Aeon of Erudition, Nous, beyond Amphoreus. It's purpose is to give birth to an Emanator of Destruction, the Lord Ravager Irontomb.
  • Khaslana and Cyrene devised a plan to create a never-ending loop of the Flamechase Journey, to eventually find the true Deliverer to lead their world into a true dawn.
  • Cyrene then shouldered the memories of each cycle, offering herself so that another Aeon can gaze into their world. But to keep the world from looping, as the Chrysos Heir of Time, she asked Phainon to kill her in every cycle, including the very cycle they are currently on.
  • As the Chrysos Heir of Worldbearing, Khaslana then set out into the cycles.

Cycle 1

  • He arrived to meet the Chrysos Heir of Law, in hopes of negotiating to stop the campaign toward Era Nova, only to be stopped by the Chrysos Heirs of Ocean, Romance, and Passage, Hysilens, Aglaea, and Tribbie.

  • Eventually, their world also ended in ruin, with Khaslana killing the Phainon (the version of him in this cycle) after he attempted of attacking him.
